About Me

I'm a 3-year experienced C/C++/C#/JavaScript coder and a graduate from Codam, the coding college in Amsterdam.


I've always had a deep passion for games, drawing, and immersing myself in books. It's incredible to think that by learning how to code, I can bring all these interests together and turn them into a playable game, and possibly share them with others. There's truly nothing more amazing than your passion into something interactive and share the joy with everyone.


About Codam

The college I attend, Codam, is part of the 42 network. Its a unique school that offers a high quality curriculum that sets clear goals to obtain and stimulates students to identify and gain the knowledge to solve problems. We're learning to learn.


Since there are no teachers, we are encouraged to think critically, innovate and solve the problems ourself. And if you can't figure it out, you ask fellow students who face the same challenges.


Because we focus purely on C in the beginning, we are able to easily transition into other languages.
