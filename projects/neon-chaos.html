NEON CHAOS

Crystal Chaos but in Neon - Designed and built at Lucky Kat Studios and published by Homa

Neon Chaos was my idea based on Crystal Chaos. Keeping the bouncing ball but reskining it in a neon and simplistic style.

We wanted to make the feel of destruction and chaos prominent, since most game ads do really well when that is the focus.

During the development I focused on the game design, the enemy AI and VFX of the weapons.
