CRYSTAL CHAOS

Satisfying ball bouncing action. Further designed and built at Lucky Kat Studios

Crystal Chaos is a really fun game where the satifsifing bouncing ball in the middle is the focus. Everytime the ball hits a edge a spell is activated.

My colleague came up with this unique idea and after creating the protoytype in 2 weeks, we received a good CPI below 1$! After that we continued to work on the game design and gameplay, adding more depth, making more levels and adding meta progression.

During the second test where we tested for retention, we also received good results. Players came back after the first couple of days. Unfortunately, the CPI rose, meaning the game was not marketable enough.

During the development I focused on the fleshing out the game design, the enemy AI, VFX of the spells, levels and balancing.
