POLAR

A super small climate game

Polar is a small game in C++ I made for the programming intake for BUAS, Breda University of Applied Sciences, with 'collect' as the theme. I passed the round, but decided to pursue a different path.


The game is about a polar bear that must collect the drops right for the environment and global warming and dodge the ones that are bad. Every drop changes the C02 ppm meter for better or worse and the ice caps melt or rise accordingly.


The game ends with the polar bear happily on ice or sad in water.


The biggest challenge was to make sure all the assets moved separately from each other, for this I used multiple threads.


All the assets were self made.
