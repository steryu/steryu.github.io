FISH FIGHT

Fish and Fight! Designed and built at Lucky Kat Studios and published by Homa

The prototype Fish Fight grew out of an idea I pitched, a game where the fish you catch is also the ammo you use to fight, with roquelike choices between the rounds.

Personally, I love fishing games, and I wanted to focus on making funny and unique looking fishes, with silly gameplay elements, like stacking it to the moon.

During the development I focused on the game design: The part where you haul in your catch and you need to choose the right multiplier gates and then the fighting itself.

After that, my teammate added nice additions to the game feel, like making the fish stack wiggle when you get hit.

Tech Stack
