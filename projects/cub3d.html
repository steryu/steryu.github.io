CUB3D

A 3D raycasting mini game named Blubble

The inspiration for this two-person team school project came from Wolfenstein 3D, the first FPS game. This project provides an opportunity to delve into the technique of ray-casting, allowing you to create a dynamic view inside a maze.


For the bonus we only made animations and a mini map. The under water themed pixel art and animation of the fishes were made by me and my partner made the mini map in the bottom right corner.

Together we made the rest of the game.

This project is only compilable on MacOs.
