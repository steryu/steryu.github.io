LOOT SNAKE

Loot Snake - A multiplayer game based on the snake toy. Designed and built at Lucky Kat Studios and published by Voodoo

Loot Snake is based on the snake toy from Slither.io, combined with extraction elements from other games.

When I first pitched the idea, I wanted to the create a game where your inventory physcially trailed behind you as a tail, and with it you can block and shield yourself form other players.

Together with my team, consisting of another developer and 2 artists we brought the idea to life. I focused on the game design, UI, weapon system and other game mechanics.

Tech Stack

C# Unity
